## 💡 Pro Tips

### Study Smarter, Not Harder
1. **Understanding > Memorization** - Know patterns, not solutions
2. **Practice regularly** - 2-3 hours daily consistency beats cramming
3. **Time yourself** - Replicate interview pressure
4. **Review mistakes** - Learn why you were wrong
5. **Teach others** - Explain concepts out loud

### Interview Day Success
1. **Ask clarifying questions** - Takes 5 min, shows intelligence
2. **Think out loud** - Let interviewer follow your logic
3. **Test thoroughly** - Use examples to verify
4. **Discuss tradeoffs** - Show system thinking
5. **Stay confident** - You've prepared well!

### Time Management
- **Easy SQL**: 10-15 minutes
- **Medium SQL**: 20-30 minutes
- **Hard SQL**: 30-45 minutes
- **Easy DSA**: 10 minutes
- **Medium DSA**: 20-25 minutes
- **Hard DSA**: 30-45 minutes
- **System Design**: 45-60 minutes
