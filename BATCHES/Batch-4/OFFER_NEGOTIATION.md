## THE NEGOTIATION CONVERSATION

### When They Make Offer

```
Scenario: HR calls with offer

WHAT THEY SAY:
"We'd like to offer you the Principal Data Engineer role
with a base salary of ₹55 lakhs and 15% annual bonus."

WHAT YOU SAY:
"Thank you! I'm excited about the opportunity.
I want to make sure we have a competitive package.
Can we schedule a brief call with HR to discuss the offer?"

WHY:
✓ Shows appreciation
✓ Sets up negotiation conversation
✓ Not negotiating on phone immediately
✗ Don't accept on the spot
✗ Don't reject immediately
```

### The Negotiation Call

```
OPENING (Your approach):

"Thank you for the offer. I'm genuinely excited about
Deutsche Börse and this role.

I want to make sure we can work together on a package
that reflects my experience and value.

I've been thinking about the role and the market.
Can we discuss the offer?"

THEIR RESPONSE (usually):
"Of course. What would you like to discuss?"

YOUR PITCH:

"Here's my thinking:

1. Experience:
   I bring 11 years in data engineering,
   including building CDM Next (petabytes, 99.99% uptime).
   This is principal-level experience.

2. Role requirements:
   This role requires leading cloud migration,
   building real-time pipeline (100K+ events/sec),
   and leading engineers. These are complex.

3. Market reality:
   Principal Data Engineer in Hyderabad: ₹60-80L range
   Your current offer: ₹55L base + 15% = ₹63.25L total
   Market middle: ₹70-75L total comp

4. My ask:
   Base: ₹65L
   Bonus: 20%
   Total: ₹78L
   This is reasonable for market and role.

5. What I bring:
   I can start immediately.
   I know your challenges (cloud migration).
   I can lead your Hyderabad team.
   Minimal ramp-up needed.

What's your flexibility here?"

KEY POINTS:
✓ Specific numbers
✓ Market justification
✓ Your value
✓ Reasonable ask
✓ Open to discussion
```

### Handling "That's our budget"

```
THEY SAY:
"Unfortunately, ₹55L is our maximum."

YOU RESPOND (Option 1 - Reasonable):
"I understand budget constraints. Let me suggest alternatives:

Could we do ₹60L base with 20% bonus? That's ₹72L total.
It's between our positions but reasonable for both sides.

Or could we structure differently:
- ₹57L base + 25% bonus = ₹71.25L
- Plus signing bonus (₹5L signing bonus = ₹62L year 1)

What's your flexibility?"

YOU RESPOND (Option 2 - Firm):
"I appreciate your constraints. However, I have
experience and value that justifies ₹65L+.

If budget is truly fixed at ₹55L, I'd need:
- 20% bonus (not 15%)
- ₹7L signing bonus
- ₹5L relocation/setup
- That gets us to comparable total comp.

Can we look at package structure?"

KEY: Be flexible on structure if not flexible on base.
```

### Handling Pushback

```
THEY SAY:
"Everyone starts at ₹50-55L, regardless of experience."

YOU RESPOND:
"I understand your leveling structure. However:
- I'm principal level, not senior
- CDM Next is ₹50L+ experience
- Market supports ₹60L+ for this role
- Replacing me at Wells Fargo would cost more in recruiting

Can we revisit this? Perhaps principal level gets more?"

THEY SAY:
"We really can't go higher. Take it or leave it."

YOU RESPOND:
"I appreciate the offer. Before I decide, let me understand:
1. Is there ANY flexibility in base, bonus, or signing bonus?
2. Can we structure differently (stock, options)?
3. Is there room for review after 6 months if I exceed expectations?

If truly no flexibility, I need to think about this."

YOUR OPTIONS:
A) Accept (probably shouldn't - you have leverage)
B) Counter with structured offer (cash, bonus, stock, sign-on)
C) Walk away (you have other options)
D) Buy time (think about it, don't decide now)
```

### Negotiating Other Elements

```
BONUS PERCENTAGE:
Initial: 15%
Target: 20%
Say: "For role criticality and performance risk, 20% is standard"

SIGNING BONUS:
Say: "Can we include ₹5L signing bonus to account for
joining costs, relocation, and transition expenses?"
Market: ₹3-5L is common

STOCK/RSUs:
If available:
Say: "Can we include stock options or RSUs?
For principal role, ₹10L+ in 4-year vesting is standard."

RELOCATION:
If you're moving within Hyderabad:
Say: "For relocation and setup (6 months), can we
include ₹2-3L relocation package?"

BENEFITS:
Say: "What's included in health insurance? Can we get
premium coverage (Amedisys Gold or similar)?"

FLEXIBILITY:
Bonus point: "Is there flexibility for review after 6-12 months
if I exceed expectations? I want to grow with the role."
```

## NEGOTIATION PRINCIPLES

### What Works

```
✓ Specific numbers (not vague)
✓ Market justification (data backing you up)
✓ Value proposition (what you bring)
✓ Professional tone (not demanding)
✓ Open to creativity (multiple package structures)
✓ Respectful throughout
✓ Friendly relationship (not adversarial)
✓ Flexibility on some points (let them win somewhere)
└─ Negotiation is win-win

EXAMPLE:
"I really appreciate the offer. I'm excited about this role.
My research shows principal engineers in this market earn ₹65-75L.
Given my experience and role requirements, I'd like to propose ₹62L base.
If that's not possible, could we add ₹3L signing bonus or increase bonus to 20%?
What works for you?"

Tone: Friendly, specific, flexible
Result: Much better response than demanding
```

### What Doesn't Work

```
✗ Ultimatums ("It's this or nothing")
✗ Threats ("I have other offers")
✗ Arrogance ("I'm worth more")
✗ Anger ("This is insulting")
✗ Long delays (looks like you don't want it)
✗ Multiple counteroffers (wastes time)
✗ Vague requests ("more money")
✗ Comparing to irrelevant jobs ("Google pays X")

These damage relationship and kill deal.

INSTEAD:
Be professional, specific, collaborative.
```

## DECISION FRAMEWORK

### When to Accept Offer

```
ACCEPT if:
✓ Base ≥ ₹55L (minimum)
✓ Total comp (with bonus) ≥ ₹63L
✓ Better than current Wells Fargo comp
✓ Principal-level role (not senior)
✓ Opportunity for growth
✓ Good company (Deutsche Börse is!)
✓ Exciting technical challenges
└─ Deutsche Börse likely meets all these

The offer is probably good. Accept it!
```

### When to Keep Negotiating

```
NEGOTIATE if:
✗ Base < ₹55L
✗ Total comp < ₹63L
✗ Not principal-level role
✗ Bonus seems low (< 15%)
✗ No signing bonus (for career move)
✗ No relocation (if applicable)

One more round. Stay professional.
```

### When to Walk Away

```
WALK AWAY if:
✗ Base < ₹50L and not willing to move
✗ They're dismissive of negotiation
✗ Dishonest (offer changes, bait and switch)
✗ Red flags about company
✗ Role isn't what was discussed
✗ You have better alternatives

You have leverage. Don't accept bad deal.
But Deutsche Börse is good company, so unlikely.
```
