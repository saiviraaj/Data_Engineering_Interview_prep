## DURING THE INTERVIEW

### First 5 Minutes (Impression Setting)

```
When you connect with interviewer:

☐ Smile and be warm
  ├─ "Hi [name], great to meet you!"
  ├─ Eye contact (into camera if video)
  ├─ Confident handshake (if in-person)
  ├─ Relaxed posture
  └─ You're happy to be there

☐ Brief small talk
  ├─ "Thanks for taking the time"
  ├─ "I'm excited to learn more about the role"
  ├─ Keep it brief (1-2 minutes)
  └─ Don't dominate with talking

☐ Ask clarifying question
  ├─ "Before we start, is the format 45 minutes?"
  ├─ "Who else is interviewing me today?"
  ├─ "What should I focus on?"
  └─ Shows you're thoughtful

The first 5 minutes set tone.
Confident, warm, professional.
You've got this! ✓
```

### During Problem (45 Minutes)

```
Follow the 7-step approach:

STEP 1 (Minutes 0-2): Understand
  ☐ Listen carefully (don't interrupt)
  ☐ Take notes
  ☐ Ask clarifying questions
  └─ Summarize: "So if I understand correctly..."

STEP 2 (Minutes 2-7): Clarify Requirements
  ☐ Ask 3-5 clarifying questions
  ├─ Scale (how many users/events)
  ├─ Latency (how fast needed)
  ├─ Consistency (eventual vs strong)
  ├─ Availability (SLA required)
  └─ Features (what must it do)

STEP 3 (Minutes 7-12): Define Data Model
  ☐ Think about key entities
  ☐ Think about access patterns
  ☐ Write them out (visualize)

STEP 4 (Minutes 12-25): High-Level Design
  ☐ Draw architecture on whiteboard
  ├─ 5-7 major components
  ├─ Clear flow/arrows
  ├─ Label everything
  └─ Explain as you draw

STEP 5 (Minutes 25-40): Deep Dive
  ☐ Pick 2-3 important components
  ☐ Discuss in detail
  ├─ Why that choice?
  ├─ What are trade-offs?
  └─ Can you scale it?

STEP 6 (Minutes 40-45): Handle Failures
  ☐ What can fail?
  ☐ How to handle?
  ☐ Recovery mechanism?

STEP 7 (Minutes 45-50): Final Questions
  ☐ "Any other areas to explore?"
  ☐ "Did I miss anything important?"
  ☐ Listen to feedback
  └─ Be ready to adjust

KEY DURING INTERVIEW:
✓ Explain out loud (don't think in silence)
✓ Check with interviewer (pauses for feedback)
✓ Use whiteboard effectively
✓ Make eye contact
✓ Stay calm
✓ Manage time
✓ Don't rush
```

### Handling Tough Situations

```
If interviewer interrupts with question:
  ☐ PAUSE what you're saying
  ☐ Listen fully
  ☐ Take moment to think
  ☐ Answer directly
  ☐ Then resume (or ask what to continue with)

If you don't know something:
  ✓ "That's a great question. I'm not 100% sure, 
     but here's my approach..."
  ✓ "I haven't worked with that specific technology,
     but here's how I'd think about it..."
  ✗ "I don't know" (seems unprepared)
  ✗ "That's not important" (seems dismissive)

If you make a mistake:
  ✓ "Wait, let me reconsider that..."
  ✓ "Actually, I think I said that wrong..."
  ✓ "Good catch, let me think through this more carefully..."
  ✗ "No, that was right" (when it wasn't)
  ✗ Getting defensive

If interviewer seems unhappy:
  ✓ "You seem concerned about that aspect"
  ✓ "What would you do differently?"
  ✓ "Help me understand your concern"
  ✗ Panic
  ✗ Getting defensive

If you run out of time:
  ✓ "We're at 45 minutes. What's most important
     to cover in remaining time?"
  ✓ Wrap up gracefully
  ✗ Rushing through
  ✗ Trying to cover everything at once
```

## AFTER THE INTERVIEW

### Immediately After (Next 15 Minutes)

```
While still on call:
  ☐ "Thank you for the time"
  ☐ "I enjoyed discussing this with you"
  ☐ "What's the next step in your process?"
  ☐ "When might I expect to hear back?"
  ☐ Get email for follow-up questions
  ☐ Professional closing

After hanging up:
  ☐ Don't panic judge yourself
  ├─ You probably did better than you think
  ├─ Most people doubt themselves
  └─ Trust your preparation

  ☐ Write down feedback
  ├─ What did they ask?
  ├─ What seemed to go well?
  ├─ What could be better?
  └─ For reflection later

  ☐ Don't contact them immediately
  ├─ Wait for them to reach out
  ├─ Unless they asked you to send something
  └─ They'll be in touch
```

## POTENTIAL ISSUES & SOLUTIONS

### Technical Issues (Remote Interview)

```
Internet drops:
  ☐ Have backup plan
  ├─ Mobile hotspot ready
  ├─ Phone number for call-in
  └─ Call them back immediately

Video/audio not working:
  ☐ Test everything before!
  ☐ Switch to phone call if needed
  ☐ "Let me switch to phone for better connection"

Screen sharing isn't working:
  ☐ Draw on paper, show to camera
  ☐ Describe what you're drawing
  ☐ Apologize briefly, move on

Running late:
  ☐ Call as soon as you know
  ☐ Apologize professionally
  ☐ Give realistic ETA
  ☐ They'll usually wait
  └─ But don't use this as excuse multiple times!
```

### Mental/Physical Issues

```
Nervous/shaking:
  ☐ Deep breathing (in through nose, out through mouth)
  ☐ Drink water (pause, collect yourself)
  ☐ Remember: Nervousness = caring about outcome
  ☐ You've prepared thoroughly
  └─ You will get through this

Blank mind (forgot something):
  ☐ Pause
  ☐ Take a breath
  ☐ "Let me think through that..."
  ☐ It will come back
  ☐ No need to panic

Dry mouth:
  ☐ Have water ready
  ☐ Take sip when needed
  ☐ No shame in taking moment

Needing bathroom break:
  ☐ Don't wait until too late
  ☐ Ask before interview starts
  ☐ "Do we have a 2-minute break?"
  ☐ Quick bathroom visit

Hunger:
  ☐ Eat good breakfast
  ☐ Have snack available
  ☐ You won't think clearly if hungry
```
