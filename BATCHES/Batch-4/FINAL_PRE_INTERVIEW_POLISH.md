## WHAT NOT TO DO IN FINAL DAYS

### Mistakes to Avoid

```
DON'T cram:
  ✗ Study heavy the night before
  ✗ Learn new concepts at last minute
  ✗ Practice multiple problems day of
  Result: Tired, overwhelmed, less sharp

DON'T doubt yourself:
  ✗ "I don't know enough"
  ✗ "What if I can't answer?"
  ✗ "I'm going to fail"
  Result: Self-sabotage, less confident

DON'T overthink:
  ✗ Analyze every possible question
  ✗ Worry about edge cases
  ✗ Fear worst-case scenarios
  Result: Analysis paralysis, anxiety

DON'T change strategy:
  ✗ Learn new frameworks day before
  ✗ Try different preparation methods
  ✗ Suddenly shift approach
  Result: Confusion, less confidence

DON'T neglect sleep:
  ✗ Stay up studying
  ✗ Stay up worrying
  ✗ Go to bed late night before
  Result: Tired, foggy, less sharp

DON'T isolate:
  ✗ Avoid talking to people
  ✗ Hide from friends/family
  ✗ Be alone with stress
  Result: Anxiety builds

DON'T catastrophize:
  ✗ "If I fail at this, my career is over"
  ✗ "This is my last chance"
  ✗ "I can't recover from a bad interview"
  Result: Unnecessary pressure
```

### What TO Do Instead

```
DO relax:
  ✓ Trust your preparation
  ✓ Rest your mind
  ✓ Get good sleep
  Result: Sharp, confident, ready

DO practice once:
  ✓ Tell your pitch once
  ✓ Explain one system once
  ✓ Then stop
  Result: Warmed up, not fatigued

DO visualize success:
  ✓ Picture yourself doing well
  ✓ Feel the confidence
  ✓ Believe in yourself
  Result: Walk in already "successful"

DO affirmations:
  ✓ "I am prepared"
  ✓ "I will do well"
  ✓ "I'm qualified"
  Result: Positive mindset

DO get support:
  ✓ Tell friends/family
  ✓ Talk to mentor
  ✓ Share excitement
  Result: Feel supported, less alone

DO care for body:
  ✓ Exercise lightly
  ✓ Eat well
  ✓ Sleep 8 hours
  Result: Body supports mind

DO trust process:
  ✓ 12 weeks of preparation
  ✓ 10+ practice problems
  ✓ Deep understanding
  Result: Genuine confidence
```
