## 💡 Why Batch 1 Is Important

These fundamentals are **critical** because:

```
Without understanding Operating Systems:
❌ Don't know why thread pools sized certain way
❌ Don't know why virtual memory is bad
❌ Don't understand I/O bottlenecks

Without understanding Databases:
❌ Don't know which database to use
❌ Don't know about indexing, sharding, replication
❌ Can't design data models

Without understanding Networking:
❌ Don't know how load balancing works
❌ Don't understand latency bottlenecks
❌ Don't know about caching headers

Without understanding Caching:
❌ Design doesn't scale (hits database too much)
❌ Misses performance opportunities
❌ Doesn't understand invalidation complexity

With Batch 1 knowledge:
✅ Can explain WHY you chose certain database
✅ Can discuss trade-offs intelligently
✅ Can analyze failure scenarios
✅ Can propose scaling solutions
✅ Can estimate system capacity
```
